# JD QA Checklist

Use this for final review or quick sweep.

## Universal

- Is the target JD surface and placement clear?
- Does the output answer the user's current goal?
- Is the core benefit visible before decoration?
- Is the tone simple, reliable, and friendly?
- Are internal terms, black-box technology claims, and agency jargon removed?
- Are facts, assumptions, and missing inputs separated?

## Copy

- Is one placement carrying one main idea?
- Does the wording match the touchpoint role?
- Does it avoid vague phrases such as high-end, youthful, breakthrough, redefine, all-in-one good goods, or must-buy when unsupported?
- Are CTA and next action clear?
- Are terms consistent across the flow?

## Expressive Marketing Copy

- Is the expressive layer eligible for this surface?
- Does the line create a clearer reason to enter, click, read, or remember?
- Is the reader emotion specific rather than generic excitement?
- Does vividness come from scene, action, contrast, or concrete benefit instead of hype?
- Does it avoid social-platform overdrama, clickbait, slang, anxiety pressure, and over-poetic abstraction?
- Can the user still understand the main benefit in about 3 seconds?

## Commerce Risk

- Are price, coupon, subsidy, rights, service promise, ranking, sales, certification, report, and efficacy claims backed by evidence?
- Are absolute terms, fake scarcity, medicalized product claims, or unsupported low-price claims removed?
- Are limitations such as rules, scope, eligibility, and settlement-page wording visible when needed?

## JOY / IP

Use this section only when the WIP JOY/IP extension is available and intended for the task.

- Is the scene type clear: fallback, empty state, abnormal page, promotion JOY, or marketing IP?
- Is JOY 2D colorful gradient where required?
- Are ears, face, body, red collar, expression, and body ratio preserved?
- Is prop count, prop style, emotion intensity, and promotion/non-promotion boundary compliant?
- Is JOY never given a tail or off-brand transformation?

## App Store Marketing Visual

Use this section only when the WIP App Store marketing extension is available and intended for the task.

- Is the type clear: regular activity, S-level activity, S-level operation, floating entrance, activity page, showcase image, or other store placement?
- Are JOY presence, 2D color JOY, ratio, title structure, subtitle length, operating element position, output format, and dimensions checked?
- Has real-device/template validation been requested when needed?

## Startup Icon / Launch Screen

Use this section only when the WIP startup icon extension is available and intended for the task.

- Is the type clear: default, long-term operation, promotion operation, launch screen, or adaptation?
- Are JD red, JOY recognition, AI star, element count, JOY ratio, small-size readability, and operation period checked?
- Are launch-screen graphic, text, and signature areas within ratio limits?
